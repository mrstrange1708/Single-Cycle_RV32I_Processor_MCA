\section{ALU Verification Evidence}
The self-checking unit testbench passed 542 vectors: 30 directed cases and
512 exhaustive combinations of operands 0--7 across all eight control
codes. Each vector checks both result and zero, including two's-complement
negative subtraction, arithmetic wraparound, and unused control codes.
A deliberately broken zero flag was rejected with a nonzero exit status.
The VCD trace and GTKWave screenshot provide the corresponding waveform
evidence. These results establish ALU behavior; full CPU program execution
and synthesized timing remain dependent on the other team modules and
their verification.
